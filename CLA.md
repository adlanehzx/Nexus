# Contributor Licence Agreement

> **This document is a standard template, adapted from the Apache Individual Contributor Licence
> Agreement 2.0.** It was prepared by engineers, not lawyers. If you intend to rely on it for a
> commercial relicensing of NEXUS, have it reviewed by a lawyer in your jurisdiction first — the
> clause that matters for that purpose is section 2, and a review costs far less than discovering
> a defect in it after accepting a hundred contributions.

## Why this exists

NEXUS is released under AGPL-3.0-or-later. Without a contributor agreement, that licence can never
change again without the individual agreement of every past contributor. This document keeps that
door open. It does **not** take your copyright away: you keep it, and you keep the right to use
your own work however you like.

If you would rather not sign, say so in your pull request. A precise bug report, a reproduction or
a design review are contributions too, and they need no agreement.

## How to sign

Comment on your pull request with exactly:

```
I have read the CLA document and I hereby sign the CLA.
```

Include the name and email address you use for `Signed-off-by:`. One signature covers all your
present and future contributions; you will not be asked again.
